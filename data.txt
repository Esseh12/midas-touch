🌿 Restock Our Hustle Initiative
Empowering Women Through Small Business Support

This initiative bridges that gap by providing practical business support that helps women continue working, earning, and sustaining their households with dignity.

Her story reflects resilience, hard work, and the importance of consistent support for women-led businesses.
👉 This initiative is ongoing, with continued follow-up to ensure beneficiaries are supported beyond initial intervention and are able to grow sustainable businesses.

1st Activity (Programme)
Elderly Women Food Support Outreach Programme – Bakin kogi Community in Bassa LGA, Plateau State
As part of our ongoing community support work, we carried out an Elderly Women Food Support Outreach Programme in Bakin Kogi community, Bassa Local Government Area of Plateau State, Nigeria.
The outreach was held in collaboration with the Village Head, Da Dr. Luka Pagyang (JP), who supported the mobilisation of beneficiaries and provided the community palace as a central meeting point for coordination and registration.
A total of 40 elderly women participated in the programme, including both widowed and non-widowed members of the community who were identified based on age and need.
Essential household food supplies and daily living support items were distributed to the beneficiaries as part of the outreach.
A special highlight of the programme was the presence of the oldest woman in the community, aged 91 years old, Yop Musa who participated in the gathering and shared in the outreach experience. Her presence reflected the deep cultural value and significance of caring for the elderly within the community.
This initiative reflects our continued commitment to reaching elderly women in underserved communities and ensuring dignity, care, and practical support at the grassroots level.




A Commitment to Care for Elderly Women – Bakin Kogi Community in Bassa LGA, Plateau State
A Mission Rooted in Care and Compassion
At the heart of this outreach is a simple but powerful value: care for elderly women.
In many rural communities, elderly women represent strength, memory, and continuity. Yet, they are also among the most overlooked in terms of consistent social support. Many live quietly with limited access to basic necessities, relying on fragile support systems within their communities.
The Elderly Women Food Support Outreach Programme in Bakin Kogi community, Bassa Local Government Area of Plateau State, was carried out as an intentional act of care, love, and dignity for elderly women.
This initiative reflects a growing commitment to ensure that elderly women are not only supported materially, but also valued, respected, and remembered.


Extending Love Through Practical Support
A total of 40 elderly women between the ages 68 – 91 years  participated in this outreach, including both widowed and non-widowed women within the community.
Essential household support items were distributed as a practical expression of care—aimed at easing daily burdens and supporting their wellbeing.
Beyond the distribution itself, the outreach created a space of warmth, connection, and recognition. It was a reminder that care is not only what is given, but also how people are seen and valued.

Honouring the Elderly in Our Communities
A deeply touching moment of the outreach was the presence of the oldest woman in the community, 91 years old, Yop Musa
Her presence represented a living history of the community and embodied resilience, grace, and longevity. She was warmly included in the gathering, symbolizing the essence of this initiative: love and care for elderly women without exception.

Volunteerism and Shared Responsibility of Care
The outreach was made possible through the dedication of volunteers from Plateau State and strong community collaboration. Their commitment reflects a shared responsibility to care for elderly women and ensure that no one is left behind in rural communities.

A Continuing Commitment
This outreach is part of a broader vision: to build a consistent culture of care for elderly women in underserved communities.
It is a reminder that meaningful impact begins with simple acts of compassion, showing up, listening, and responding to real human needs with dignity and love.
With continued support and partnership, more elderly women across rural communities can be reached, supported, and embraced within this circle of care.
Photo & Video Documentation
The images and videos from this outreach reflect authentic field documentation captured during the programme in a remote rural community setting. They were taken on-site during active engagement with beneficiaries and community leaders.
The materials represent real-time moments of the outreach, preserving the natural setting and atmosphere in which the programme took place.


2nd Activity( Programme)
Why the “Restock Her Hustle Initiative” Matters
Empowering Women Through Small Business Support
The Restock Her Hustle Initiative is an ongoing women-focused empowerment programme created to support hardworking female small business owners who are striving every day to provide for themselves and their families despite limited financial resources. When restocking becomes difficult, income becomes unstable. When income becomes unstable, families feel the impact.

Across many communities, women carry the responsibility of sustaining entire households through small businesses, roadside trading, and daily income activities. These businesses are often not just sources of income, but the foundation for food, education, and family survival.
Yet, for many of these women, one major challenge continues to stand in the way of stability and growth, the inability to consistently restock goods and sustain daily business operations.

Bridging the Gap Between Hard Work and Stability
The Restock Her Hustle Initiative was created to bridge this gap.
It provides practical, business-focused support that helps hardworking women continue trading, earning, and sustaining their livelihoods with dignity. Rather than offering temporary relief alone, the initiative focuses on strengthening the ability of women to remain active in their businesses and build more stable sources of income.
Sometimes, all a hardworking woman needs is not just encouragement, but the opportunity and support to continue her hustle without interruption.

A Continuing Commitment
This is an ongoing programme.
This initiative is rooted in the belief that economic empowerment for women strengthens entire communities and to help women build stronger businesses that can continue supporting their families and improving their quality of life over time.
We remain committed to reaching more women in need, supporting their businesses, and walking alongside them as they grow. Beneficiaries are not only supported once, but are followed up to better understand their progress, challenges, and growth over time. Our goal is:  To ensure that no hardworking woman is left behind in her journey to build a stable livelihood 




Building Stronger Women. Stronger Families. Stronger Communities.
The Restock Her Hustle Initiative continues to stand as a commitment to practical empowerment, supporting hardworking women not only to survive, but to grow, thrive, and stand stronger in their businesses and their families.
Because when women are empowered economically-
Entire communities rise with them.
Families are better able to thrive, 
Children’s needs are more consistently met, 

Featured Beneficiary – Ms. Valentina
Meet one of the beneficiaries of the initiative is Ms. Valentina, a hardworking roadside food vendor supported through the Restock Her Hustle Initiative.
Valentina sells noodles daily to support herself and her family. Like many small business owners, one of her major challenges was access to consistent capital for restocking.
She shared that there were times she relied on collecting goods on credit to keep her business running, and when credit was unavailable, sustaining daily sales became increasingly difficult.
Through this initiative, her business was restocked with the products she sells most frequently, helping her continue operations with greater stability and confidence.
Additional support was also provided to assist with daily business needs and reduce immediate financial pressure connected to running her small business. 
For her, this support represented more than restocking, it was encouragement, stability, and renewed hope.


3rd Activity ( Programme)

Skills for Independence Programme 


From Learning to Leadership: The Story of Bridget Ishaku
A Journey of Skills, Service, and Independence
The story of Bridget Ishaku is a powerful reflection of what happens when women are given the opportunity to grow, learn, and be supported through intentional empowerment.
Bridget’s journey began with a strong desire to acquire a vocational skill that would provide her with long-term independence and stability. Recognising this aspiration, she was supported to undertake professional training in hairdressing, makeup, and nail technology in Plateau State.
With determination and commitment, she fully embraced her training and developed her skills over time, progressing steadily toward professional certification.

A Heart for Service Through Volunteering
Even during her training period, Bridget chose to give back.
She actively volunteered with the organisation during community outreach programmes, including the Elderly Women Food Support Outreach Programme in Plateau State, where she supported coordination and field activities alongside community leaders.
Her involvement reflected not only personal growth, but also a developing sense of responsibility, compassion, and leadership within her community work.

Graduation and New Beginnings
Bridget has now successfully completed her vocational training and officially graduated, marking an important milestone in her personal empowerment journey.
Her achievement represents more than certification, it reflects resilience, dedication, and the transformation that comes through access to skills development opportunities.

From Training to Entrepreneurship
Following her graduation, Bridget has taken a bold step into entrepreneurship by establishing her own beauty business in Jos Plateau State, Nigeria, offering hairdressing, makeup, and nail services.
To support her transition into business ownership, the organisation contributed by covering her first-year shop rent, helping her secure a stable foundation to begin her entrepreneurial journey. In addition, basic shop setup and improvement works were also supported to enhance the functionality and presentation of the space, including light refurbishment and aesthetic improvements to create a conducive environment for her business.
This support was provided to ensure that her skills translate into sustainable income and long-term independence.

A Story of Empowerment in Action
Bridget’s journey reflects the heart of our mission — empowering women through opportunity, skills development, and sustainable support systems.
Her story demonstrates what is possible when women are not only trained but also encouraged, supported, and trusted to grow into independence.
From learning, to volunteering, to entrepreneurship, Bridget now stands as an example of transformation through empowerment and a reminder that investment in women creates lasting impact far beyond the individual.
