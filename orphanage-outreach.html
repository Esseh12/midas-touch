Caring for Children at God’s Mercy Orphanage

Every child deserves care, nourishment, and the chance to learn. As part of our commitment to supporting vulnerable children, Gentle Midas Impact Foundation carried out an outreach to Aduke Olaidibo God’s Mercy Orphanage — bringing food, personal-care items, educational supplies, and financial support to the children in the home.

Working alongside the caregivers who look after the children every day, we focused on what the home needed most: nourishing food to keep the children well-fed, hygiene and personal-care items for their daily wellbeing, and learning materials to support their education. A cash donation was also provided to help the orphanage meet its ongoing needs.

What We Provided

Items Donated

  • 1 bag of rice
  • 3 big-size Vaseline
  • 6 pieces of Soulmate cream
  • 8 big packs of sanitary pads
  • 1 big-size whiteboard
  • 13 rolls and 9 pieces of Golden Morn
  • 6 dozens of markers
  • 1 bottle of ink
  • 2 cartons of A4 paper
  • 6 packs of Maggi (100 cubes) and 4 sachets of 25 pieces
  • ₦100,000 cash support

A Note of Appreciation

Following the outreach, the caregivers at the orphanage sent us a heartfelt letter of appreciation — a reminder that even small acts of care leave a lasting mark.

Aduke Olaidibo God’s Mercy Orphanage

“This is to express our deep appreciation for your love, unflinching support, and donations. We hereby acknowledge your kindness and generosity towards the children.

God bless you, Sir/Ma. Thank you for your support, and may the Lord bless and increase you on every side.”

Best regards to your family.
